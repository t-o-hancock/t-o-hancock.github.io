Thomas O. Hancock

Senior Research Fellow, Choice Modelling Centre & Institute for Transport Studies, University of Leeds.

I am an interdisciplinary behavioural modeller and mathematical psychologist. I build psychologically grounded mathematical models of how people make choices across a wide range of contexts, bringing together ideas from across disciplines to understand and predict behavioural change.
